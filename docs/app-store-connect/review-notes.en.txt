Lucky Island is a single-player island-building strategy game with 15 levels. No sign-in, subscription, or in-app purchase is required. Gameplay works offline; viewing the externally hosted Privacy Policy requires internet access.

HOW TO REVIEW
1. Launch the app and tap "Let's begin".
2. On Island, tap "Start challenge" and confirm.
3. Collect 20 Wood in the first level using "Spin the wheel".
4. Open the wheel workshop to replace a tile. The first replacement in each new challenge is free; later replacements use Coins earned in the game.
5. Choose an upgrade every three spins or when landing on a Chest.
6. Complete levels to unlock landmarks, achievements, additional challenges, and wheel styles. No payment is required to unlock content.
7. Visit Collection to view unlocks. Settings includes How to play, Rules and odds, Quick spins, Privacy Policy, Save Information, and Reset all progress.

MECHANICS
All eight wheel tiles have equal 12.5% odds. The wheel grants in-game resources and effects. There is no wagering of money or Coins on spin outcomes, no paid spins, no cash-out, and no monetary prizes. Coins cannot be purchased or exchanged for money.

SAVES AND SETTINGS
Progress saves locally. If the app closes during a spin, continuing preserves the saved result rather than drawing again. The app has no developer-operated cloud-sync service. Device backups may include saves according to the user's system settings.

Enable Settings > Quick spins for shorter animations. Settings > Reset all progress starts a fresh game after confirmation.

PRIVACY
The native game has no advertising or analytics SDKs. Settings > Privacy Policy opens the published policy in the system browser. Save Information explains local saves and reset behavior.
