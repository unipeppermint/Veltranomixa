This policy applies to the current single-player, offline features of Lucky Island (the “App”). It explains how game data is handled and how you can manage it.

1. Overview

The App does not require an account or login and can be played offline. The current version has no advertising, in-app purchases, social features, user analytics services, or developer-operated game servers.

The App does not proactively send your progress, settings, or personal information to us, and does not track you across apps or websites. You do not need to provide your name, phone number, email address, or other identity information to play.

2. Data stored on your device

To save progress and resume a game, the App stores the following within its own storage on your device:

  • Your current level, resources, remaining spins, wheel layout, upgrades, and workshop changes;
  • Previously generated spin results and random identifiers used to prevent duplicate rewards; these identifiers are not used to identify or track users;
  • Completed levels, landmarks, collectibles, achievements, and cumulative in-game statistics;
  • Preferences such as sound, haptics, quick spins, appearance, and tutorial status.

This data supports local gameplay. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or remote behavioral analytics. The App keeps a local backup to help recover a damaged save. Unreadable saves may also be retained locally for troubleshooting; they are not automatically uploaded.

3. Permissions and third-party components

The current version does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or advertising tracking permission, and does not use an advertising identifier. Playing sound effects does not require microphone access.

The App uses SnapKit for on-device interface layout. The integrated component is not used to collect or upload user data. The App does not integrate advertising, third-party behavioral analytics, or automatic crash-report uploading SDKs.

4. Sharing and platform services

The App does not sell, rent, or provide local game data to advertisers, or transmit it to game servers operated by us or other third parties.

Apple handles platform services such as App Store downloads, system diagnostics, and device backups under its own policies and your settings. The App does not provide its own cloud-sync service. However, depending on your device settings, saves may be included in iCloud Backup or computer backups. We cannot access these backups through the App. You can manage them through your Apple device or computer settings.

For Apple's data practices, see the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Retention, reset, and deletion

Local game data stays on your device so you can continue playing, until you reset or delete the App's data.

In the App, go to Settings → Reset all progress and confirm to clear the current run, long-term progress, and preferences. This resets both the main save and its local backup and removes damaged-save copies retained by the App. The App creates an empty save so you can start again.

You can also use iOS's Delete App option to remove the App and its local data. Offload App generally keeps documents and data and is not equivalent to deletion. Resetting progress or deleting the App does not actively remove existing iCloud or computer backups; manage those separately in the relevant backup settings.

Because we do not receive your local game saves, we cannot remotely view, export, restore, or delete your progress. The App has no accounts to close.

6. Information you choose to send us

If you email the contact listed in this policy, we receive your sender email address, message, and any screenshots or attachments you choose to provide. We use this information only to respond to inquiries, handle privacy requests, or troubleshoot reported issues, not for advertising or marketing.

Correspondence is processed by the email services used to send and receive it. We retain it only as long as needed to handle the request, unless applicable law requires longer retention. You may use the same contact to request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted. Please do not send sensitive personal information unrelated to your request.

7. Children's privacy

The App does not proactively request personal identity information from any player, including children, and does not offer public chat or personal profiles.

If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent unnecessary personal information through support email, they may contact us to request its deletion. Use of the App should follow its App Store age rating and appropriate parental guidance.

8. Data protection

The App uses iOS application storage isolation for local data, along with a local backup and atomic writes to reduce the risk of save corruption. We recommend protecting access to your device and backups. No storage method is completely secure, and a local backup cannot guarantee recovery of all lost progress.

9. Updates and contact

If the App's features or data practices change, we will update this policy and its revision date. For material changes, we will provide appropriate notice and obtain any separately required consent where applicable.
